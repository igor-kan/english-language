# English Mastery 📚

A comprehensive, minimalist Quarto publishing portal dedicated to mastering the English language—from foundational grammar and etymology to high-stakes communication, principled negotiation, and dialectical debate.

🌐 **Live Website:** [https://igor-kan.github.io/english-language](https://igor-kan.github.io/english-language)

---

## 🏛️ Curriculum Structure

* **Grammar (`grammar/`):** Syntax, morphology, parts of speech, tense-aspect paradigms, and clause coordination.
* **Vocabulary (`vocabulary/`):** Greco-Roman root decomposition, analytical diction, and context-driven collocations.
* **Communication (`communication/`):** The Minto Pyramid Principle, active listening, executive prose, and conversational attunement.
* **Negotiation (`negotiation/`):** Harvard principled negotiation, BATNA cultivation, and FBI tactical empathy.
* **Debate & Logic (`debate/`):** Toulmin argumentation framework, fallacy deconstruction, and refutation mechanics.
